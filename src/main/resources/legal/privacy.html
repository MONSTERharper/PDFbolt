What we do

PDFbolt (mypdfbolt.shop) provides online PDF tools. When you use a Live tool, you upload files to our server so we can process them and return a download.

Your files

Uploaded files are used only to run the tool you requested. We do not keep your files on disk after processing finishes. Do not upload content you are not allowed to share with us.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, and Google AdSense may show ads. These services may set cookies. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Contact form

If you contact us, we receive the name, email, subject, and message you send so we can reply.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold. Contact us via the Contact page.
