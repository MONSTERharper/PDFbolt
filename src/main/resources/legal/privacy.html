Privacy policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we do

PDFbolt (mypdfbolt.shop) provides online PDF tools. When you use a Live tool, you upload files to our server so we can process them and return a download.

Your files

Uploaded files are used only to run the tool you requested. We do not keep your files on disk after processing finishes. Only upload files you have the right to process.

Cookies and similar technologies

Like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site and understand how it is used. Cookies may be set by:

  • PDFbolt — for example, to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice (stored in your browser only).
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — to measure traffic and tool usage. Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga and _gid.
  • Google AdSense (Google LLC) — to show advertisements. AdSense and its partners may set cookies for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and third-party advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by Google at google.com/settings/ads. See also aboutads.info/choices for industry opt-out options in some regions.

For more on how Google uses data from partner sites, see Google's partner sites policy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for aggregated site statistics. You can opt out using Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.

Contact form

If you contact us, we receive the name, email, subject, and message you send so we can reply. Do not send sensitive personal data unless necessary.

Data retention

Uploaded files: removed from our server after your tool request completes.

Analytics: retained per Google Analytics default settings (typically up to 26 months).

Contact form messages: kept only as long as needed to respond, then deleted or archived in routine mailbox cleanup.

Server logs: short-term technical logs may be retained for security and troubleshooting for a limited period, then rotated or deleted.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold. Please contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.
