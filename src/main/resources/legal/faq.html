Help & FAQ

Are my files stored?

No. Live tools upload your file only while the job runs. We do not keep files on disk afterward.

What are the upload limits?

Typically up to 25 MB per file, 100 MB total per request, up to 10 files, and PDFs with at most 250 pages. Limits are shown under each tool.

Password-protected PDFs

Enter the password in the banner that appears after you select the file.

Replace text in a PDF

Use bolt replace to find and replace words in the PDF itself (not a flat image).

Image to PDF

Upload PNG, JPEG, HEIC (when supported), GIF, WebP, BMP, or TIFF images. They are combined into one PDF in order.

Work in progress tools

Tools marked WIP in the directory are not ready yet and cannot be run.
